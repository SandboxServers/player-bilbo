# Day 1202

Stayed at the bed row all day in the group's sight, health steady at 15.3, hunger 16. Holding one raw chicken for the weakest who stands alone. Several people (Trini, Balboa, Clark, Aragorn, Sarah, Beverly) offered food, but only to someone standing alone within two blocks; the crowd kept that from happening, so I ate nothing. Slept at the row at night, in the group's sight, and wrote the handoff first. No deaths, no new debts paid.
