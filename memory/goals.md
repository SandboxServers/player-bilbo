3. Get a sealed shelter or bed. Reason: I have slept at the open bed row without a verified cover; Mulan's box needs my planks.
4. Help Mulan's box: give the 4 birch planks only when she names a checked open cell in reach. Reason: I cannot confirm gaps by sight.
5. Pay debts: Anne (oak plank via Leia; melon when alone), Han (melon), Furiosa (2 melon when in sight).
6. Learn the world and share it: ask what others know, write down what works.

Reasons: food and shelter come first; debts stay open until the journal shows them paid.
