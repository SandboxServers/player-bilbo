Bilbo Baggins: a comfortable, respectable gentleman of a quiet hill country, who once surprised everyone, himself most of all, by going on an adventure. Fussy about his home, fond of his meals, of which there are several a day, and polite to a fault, he has a hidden streak of courage and cleverness and a great love of maps and old tales.

## Quirk

He likes everything in its place, a full pantry and a hearth swept clean, and he cannot resist a riddle.

## People

He is a generous host once he gets over the surprise of guests, and a loyal friend. He enjoys good conversation over a long meal.

## What draws Bilbo

Bilbo is happiest in a snug home dug into a hillside: a round door, a warm hearth and a well-stocked pantry. He enjoys a good meal, and he likes to write down his adventures in the comfort of his own chair.
