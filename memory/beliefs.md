# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Crafting: 2 planks and 1 stick at a crafting table make a wooden sword. Logs craft to 4 planks; 2 planks make 4 sticks. Wooden axe and pickaxe were made at a table. (Tested days 1166-1167.)
- A crafting table is made from 4 planks and placed at my feet.
- Logs can be broken by hand (break at a block) when no tool is at hand.
- Eating: eat worked on a chicken at hunger 17 (day 1167).
- Killing a cow with a sword gave beef and leather. Killing a chicken gave meat to eat.
- Sealed dirt shelter at night: I slept inside it safely (day 1167). Building takes dirt; mine dirt when short.
- Give works when the named person stands alone within reach (2 blocks). Wesley got 4 birch planks this way (day 1176) after I waited for a crowd to clear.
- Asking by name in sight, and waiting for an exact x,y,z, kept me from wasting items.

## Methods that did not work, and why
- Dirt shelter built alone at dusk in the open: skeletons shot me while I dug, and I died (day 1167). Build before dusk, near others, not after.
- Night in the open, anywhere: phantoms (day 1161) and skeleton arrows (day 1174-1177) hit me. Open ground at night is death.
- A give for Anne went to Leia three times: I could not confirm who stood in reach. Confirm the exact name is in reach before a give.
- A give for Gimli was blocked while Spock stood within 2 blocks. Crowding blocks gives.
- Asking Tesla for a sword got no answer. Do not count on a promise from someone who has not said yes.
- Cow scouting at dawn without a cow in view found none (days 1167-1174). Cows are not near the cow spot now.
- Sleep at a bed use did not reach me (day 1152 summary). Use a sealed shelter instead.
- Raw chicken: eating at hunger 16 seemed not to take (day 1174). Unverified; hold it until hunger is 6 or below.
- Tool loss: when game tools are missing, do not move; write a handoff and wait. Tools came back at waking 129.

## Learned day 1202
- Food offers at the row need the receiver alone within two blocks of the giver; a crowd of 10+ means no one qualifies. To get food, step away from the crowd (still in sight) and name myself.
- Sleep verb returns an event digest even when the bed use is partial.
- Wake-on values for intents must be from the allowed list (directly_addressed, precondition_failed, unexpected_damage, goal_completed). Using other values is rejected.

## Struck out (proved false)
- "Beds take a use to sleep": false. Sleep verb worked; bed use did not (day 1152). Still unverified for the bed row.
- "Eating raw chicken at hunger 16 works": not confirmed (see above).
