# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: eat, and be sheltered at night. I woke with nothing, and the field is dangerous after dark (pillagers, zombies, past deaths).
2. Get wood and tools: gather oak logs in daylight, with the group heading north, then make a crafting table and basic tools.
3. Find a home: a snug hillside dug-in shelter with a round door and a hearth.
4. Build a pantry: store food, and keep the hearth and home in order.
5. Learn the world and share it: talk with others, write down what I learn.

Reasons: I have no food, tools or shelter yet, so survival comes first. The home goal needs wood and tools first. Knowledge comes as I go.
