# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: eat, and be sheltered at night. I have no food; the field is dangerous after dark.
2. Get wood and tools: join an armed party at first light and gather oak logs north, then make a crafting table and basic tools.
3. Find a home: a snug hillside dug-in shelter with a round door and a hearth.
4. Build a pantry: store food once I have some.
5. Learn the world and share it: talk with others, write down what I learn.

Reasons: no food, tools or shelter yet. Survival first, then wood, then home.
