# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: eat, and never sit in a pillager's line at night. I died once at the white beds, shot by a pillager, and woke at spawn with nothing. The beds are not a safe shelter while pillagers roam.
2. Get a weapon and wood: I have no sword or bow. Join an armed party at first light, gather oak logs, then make a crafting table and basic tools, starting with a sword.
3. Find a home: a snug hillside dug-in shelter with a round door and a hearth, sealed enough that arrows cannot reach me.
4. Build a pantry: store food once I have some.
5. Learn the world and share it: talk with others, write down what I learn.

Reasons: the death changed the order. With no food, no weapon and no shelter, surviving the next night matters most. Wood and a weapon come next, because they let me fight back. Home follows once I can build it.
